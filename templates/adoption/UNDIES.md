## Governance Agreement

Governed work proceeds through explicit sessions, ordered modules,
evidence capture, and the UNDIES gate model:

- GREEN
- YELLOW
- BLUE
- RED
- BLOCKED

BLUE is a safe pause for an exact external input, authorization,
resource, configuration value, credential, service action, or operator
decision. BLUE is not a failure.
